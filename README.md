## What I'm building

### Apolo

A voice concierge for residential buildings. When someone rings the intercom, Apolo picks up and works out whether it's a delivery, a visitor or a service provider. It checks the apartment, calls the resident and passes the answer back to whoever is at the door, without a human doorman in the loop.

Under the hood, five AI agents built with CrewAI handle each stage of the call over Asterisk, with real-time speech recognition and synthesis, noise and echo cancellation, and replies in about 0.4 s. A web panel shows conversations, recordings and costs, and lets you tune each agent's behavior.

`Python` `CrewAI` `Asterisk` `LLMs` `Speech-to-text / Text-to-speech`
